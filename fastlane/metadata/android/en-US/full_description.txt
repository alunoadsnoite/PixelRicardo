PixelRicardo is an offline creature-collecting game with a Brazilian Portuguese interface. Explore a pixel world, catch 24 species, care for creatures and follow their evolutions and achievements.

Includes three difficulty levels, character outfits, daily quests, synthesized music and sound effects. No ads or in-app purchases.

Export and import progress using BACKUP on the title screen. Import replaces current progress after confirmation. Keep backups outside the app before uninstalling.

The interface is Portuguese only. No account or internet connection is required.
